Solitaire at work: free Klondike solitaire that looks like a task board

A hidden solitaire game for the office. The cards are tickets on a team's task board, so it looks like work while you play. Classic Klondike solitaire, free in your browser with no ads, no download and no sign up.

How to play

  • Move every card to the four Done lists, one per suit, from ace up to king.
  • In the seven columns, build down in alternating colors: hearts and diamonds go on spades and clubs, and spades and clubs go on hearts and diamonds. Only a king can go in an empty column.
  • Click a card to pick it up, then click where it goes, or drag it. Places it can go are highlighted. Double-click a card to send it to Done.
  • Click the Deck to turn over three cards (or one, with Draw 1). When the Deck runs out, click it to start it over.
  • Cards have no suit symbols: each one is named, like eight-hearts (8H), and colored like real cards: hearts and diamonds red, spades and clubs black.
