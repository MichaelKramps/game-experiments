About Kramps Games

Kramps is my name and I like to play and make games.

Kramps Games are games that look like work. These games are intended to be played in a browser on your computer.

The idea of these games is that they could be played in any context, even at work, and it would appear as though you are working. This isn’t to say that I built these games to discourage people from doing their jobs. In my career as a software engineer I’ve been in many situations where work did not require my constant undivided attention: eating my lunch at my desk, waiting for unit tests to run, sitting in a company wide meeting, and any number of other activities where there’s no reason I couldn’t be entertaining myself.

That’s where Kramps Games come in. They are all turn based and can be left unattended at any moment, for any length of time. And the games look like common computer tasks like operating a terminal, navigating a file system, reading through emails, or maybe even moving tasks on a sprint board. Just pop into a game for a minute or two, or for your entire lunch break.

All my games are free of advertisements and require no sign up.
