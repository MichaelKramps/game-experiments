Spreadsheet Minesweeper: Minesweeper in an Excel-style spreadsheet

A free Minesweeper game that looks like a spreadsheet, so it looks like work while you play. The board is a range of cells in a budget sheet, with a dash in every cell you haven't revealed. Turn on Shading for an easier-to-read board. Play it in your browser with no download, no ads and no sign up.

How to play

  • Hidden cells show a dash. Click one to reveal it. Your first click is always safe.
  • A revealed number counts the mines in the 8 cells around it. A 0 has none, and opens its neighbors for you.
  • Right-click a hidden cell to flag it as a mine. On a phone, long-press it or turn on Flag mode.
  • Click a number that already has that many flags around it to reveal the rest of its neighbors.
  • Reveal every cell that isn't a mine to clear the sheet. Reveal a mine and the game is over.
  • Keyboard: arrow keys move, Enter or Space reveals, F flags.

Sizes: Beginner (9 by 9, 10 mines), Intermediate (16 by 16, 40 mines) and Expert (30 by 16, 99 mines), or pick a custom size up to 30 by 24.
