Spreadsheet Word Search: word search puzzles in an Excel-style spreadsheet

A free word search game that looks like a spreadsheet, so it looks like work while you play. The letters fill a range of cells and the words to find are listed in a column beside them. Play it in your browser with no download, no ads and no sign up.

How to play

  • Each puzzle hides 12 words from one theme, like Office Supplies, Meeting Notes, Animals or Space. There are 40 themes.
  • Drag across a word to select it, or click its first letter and then its last. A word you find gets highlighted and checked off the list.
  • Words run in any direction: across, down, diagonally and backwards, and they cross each other. Easy is a 10 by 10 grid, Medium 12 by 12 and Hard 15 by 15.
  • Find all 12 to finish. Your best time on each level is saved.

More classic games that look like work: Spreadsheet Minesweeper, Spreadsheet Sudoku, Spreadsheet 2048, Spreadsheet Chess, Yacht dice, Spades at work, Spreadsheet Four in a Row, Spreadsheet Mancala and Solitaire at work.
