Spreadsheet Mancala: the mancala board game in an Excel-style spreadsheet

A free mancala game that looks like a spreadsheet, so it looks like work while you play. The pits and stores are cells and the seeds are numbers in them. Play the computer in your browser with no download, no ads and no sign up.

How to play

  • Each side has six pits and a store. Your pits are along the bottom and your store is on the right. Every pit starts with 4 seeds, or any number from 1 to 10.
  • On your turn, pick one of your pits. Its seeds are sown one at a time, counter-clockwise, into the next pits and your store, skipping the computer's store.
  • If the last seed lands in your store, you go again. If it lands in an empty pit on your side and the pit across has seeds, you capture them both into your store.
  • When one side runs out of seeds, each player keeps what is left on their side. The bigger store wins.
  • Play the computer on Easy, Medium or Hard. Every move is logged on the Moves sheet, and your record is kept for each level.
