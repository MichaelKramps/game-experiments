Spreadsheet games that look like Excel

Free classic games played in a spreadsheet, so they look like work while you play. The boards are ranges of cells, the moves are logged on their own sheet, and there's a formula bar, sheet tabs and a status bar like any other spreadsheet. They're turn based, so you can stop at any moment. Play in your browser with no download, no ads and no sign up.

  • Spreadsheet Minesweeper: clear the sheet without revealing a mine. Minesweeper in a spreadsheet that looks like Excel.
  • Spreadsheet Sudoku: fill the sheet so every row, column and block has 1 to 9, with new puzzles at four levels.
  • Spreadsheet 2048: slide and add numbers up to 2048 in the cells, then keep going.
  • Spreadsheet Chess: chess against the computer on an A1:H8 board, easy to hard.
  • Spreadsheet Four in a Row: drop X's down the columns and line up four before the computer.
  • Spreadsheet Mancala: sow seeds around the board and fill your store, with the seeds as numbers.
  • Spreadsheet Word Search: find 12 hidden words in a sheet of letters, in 40 themes.
  • Yacht: the classic five dice game, with dice as files and the score sheet in a spreadsheet.
  • Spades at work: partner with the computer, with cards as files and the scorecard in a spreadsheet.

More games that look like work: Solitaire at work on a task board, diff guess key, a word game that looks like a terminal, and Ctrl+Alt+Deal, a poker deckbuilder. All Kramps Games.
