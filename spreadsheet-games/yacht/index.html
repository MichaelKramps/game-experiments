Yacht: the five dice game, with a score sheet that looks like a spreadsheet

A free Yahtzee-style dice game that looks like work. Your dice are files in a file browser and your score sheet is an Excel-style spreadsheet. Play it in your browser with no download, no ads and no sign up.

How to play

  • Each game is 13 turns. On each turn, roll five dice up to three times.
  • After the first roll, pick the dice you want to roll again and roll; the rest stay.
  • Then score the dice in one empty category of the score sheet: Ones to Sixes, Three of a kind, Four of a kind, Full house (25), Small straight (30), Large straight (40), Yacht (five alike, 50) or Chance.
  • Score 63 or more in Ones to Sixes for a 35 point bonus. Each extra Yacht after a scored Yacht is worth 100 more.

Yahtzee is a trademark of Hasbro. Yacht is the older public domain dice game and is not affiliated with Hasbro.
