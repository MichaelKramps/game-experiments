Spreadsheet Four in a Row: the four in a row game in an Excel-style spreadsheet

A free Connect Four-style game that looks like a spreadsheet, so it looks like work while you play. The board is a range of cells, you are X and the computer is O. Play it in your browser with no download, no ads and no sign up.

How to play

  • The board is 7 columns wide and 6 rows tall. Click a column to drop your X into the lowest empty cell.
  • Get four in a row across, down or diagonally before the computer does. If the board fills up first, it's a draw.
  • Play the computer on Easy, Medium or Hard. You can go first, let the computer go first, or take turns.
  • Undo takes back your last move. Every move is logged on the Moves sheet, and your record is kept for each level.
