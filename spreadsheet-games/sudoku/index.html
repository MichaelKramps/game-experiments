Spreadsheet Sudoku: Sudoku in an Excel-style spreadsheet

A free Sudoku game that looks like a spreadsheet, so it looks like work while you play. The puzzle is a range of cells, with sums at the end of every row and column. Play it in your browser with no download, no ads and no sign up.

How to play

  • Fill the range A1:I9 so every row, every column and every 3×3 block has the numbers 1 to 9 once.
  • Click a cell and type a number. Backspace clears it. On a phone, tap a cell, then a number.
  • Given numbers are black and yours are blue. A number that repeats in a row, column or block turns red.
  • Turn on Notes to pencil in possible numbers. Undo takes back your last change.
  • Levels: Easy, Medium, Hard and Expert. Every puzzle has exactly one answer.
