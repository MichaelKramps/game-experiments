Spades at work: the partnership card game, with cards as files and a spreadsheet scorecard

A free Spades card game that looks like work. Your 13 cards are files in a file browser and every trick is logged in a spreadsheet. You and a computer partner play against two computer opponents. Play it in your browser with no download, no ads and no sign up.

How to play

  • Each player gets 13 cards and bids how many tricks they will take. You and your partner add your bids together.
  • Follow the suit that was led if you can. Spades are trump and beat every other suit. You can't lead spades until one has been played.
  • Make your team's bid to score 10 points per trick bid, plus 1 per extra trick. Extra tricks are bags, and every 10 bags costs 100 points. Missing your bid loses 10 points per trick bid.
  • Bid nil to take no tricks at all for 100 points (lose 100 if you take one). When your team is behind by 100 or more you can bid blind nil before looking at your cards, for 200.
  • The first team to 500 points wins. A setting lets you play your partner's hand too.
