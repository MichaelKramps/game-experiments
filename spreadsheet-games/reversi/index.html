Spreadsheet Reversi: the Reversi board game in an Excel-style spreadsheet

A free Othello-style game that looks like a spreadsheet, so it looks like work while you play. The board is a range of cells, you are X and the computer is O. Play it in your browser with no download, no ads and no sign up.

How to play

  • The board is 8 by 8, the range A1:H8. It starts with two X's and two O's crossed in the middle.
  • Place an X so that a straight line of O's, across, down or diagonally, sits between it and another X. Those O's flip to X. Every move has to flip at least one piece.
  • If you have no move that flips anything, your turn is passed. The game ends when neither side can move, and whoever has more pieces wins.
  • Hover over an empty cell to see whether you can play there. Play the computer on Easy, Medium or Hard, and go first, second or take turns.
  • Undo takes back your last move. Every move is logged on the Moves sheet, and your record is kept for each level.
