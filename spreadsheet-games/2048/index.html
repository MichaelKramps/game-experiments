Spreadsheet 2048: the 2048 puzzle in an Excel-style spreadsheet

A free 2048 game that looks like a spreadsheet, so it looks like work while you play. The tiles are plain numbers in a range of cells. Play it in your browser with no download, no ads and no sign up.

How to play

  • Use the arrow keys (or W A S D) to slide every number on the board. On a phone, swipe.
  • Two equal numbers that slide into each other add up into one: 2 and 2 make 4, 4 and 4 make 8. A new number appears after every move.
  • You get one undo per game to take back a move.
  • Reach 2048 to win, then continue to go for a higher score, or start a new game.
  • Turn on Color scale to shade the numbers by size, like conditional formatting.
