Spreadsheet Chess: play chess against the computer in an Excel-style spreadsheet

A free chess game that looks like a spreadsheet, so it looks like work while you play. The board is a range of cells in a budget sheet and the moves are logged on a second sheet tab. Play the computer in your browser with no download, no ads and no sign up.

How to play

  • Click one of your pieces, then click the cell it should move to. Dots show where it can go.
  • Pick Easy, Medium or Hard (rated about 2000), or enter a custom rating for the computer from 1320 to 3190. Choose whether you play White or Black.
  • The pieces are letter codes (K, Q, R, B, N, P), like data in a sheet. The Pieces menu can show them as point values instead (pawn 1, knight 3, bishop 3.1, rook 5, queen 9, king 100, with Black's negative), so the board looks like a table of numbers, or as chess icons.
  • Undo takes back your last move. Keyboard: arrow keys move, Enter or Space picks and moves, Ctrl+Z undoes, F2 starts a new game.
  • All the standard rules apply: castling, en passant, promotion, and draws by stalemate, repetition, the 50-move rule and insufficient material.

The computer opponent is Stockfish, the open-source chess engine, running in your browser.
