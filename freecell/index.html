FreeCell at work: free FreeCell solitaire that looks like a task board

A hidden FreeCell game for the office. The cards are tickets on a team's task board, so it looks like work while you play. Classic FreeCell solitaire with the standard numbered deals, free in your browser with no ads, no download and no sign up.

How to play

  • All 52 cards are dealt face up into eight columns. Move every card to the four Done lists, one per suit, from ace up to king.
  • In the columns, build down in alternating colors: hearts and diamonds go on spades and clubs, and spades and clubs go on hearts and diamonds. Any card can go in an empty column.
  • The four Cells each hold one card while you dig for the card you need. A run of cards moves together if there are enough free Cells and empty columns to move it one card at a time.
  • Every deal is numbered from 1 to 32000, the standard FreeCell numbering, so you can replay a deal or play one someone told you about. Every deal can be solved except game 11982.
  • Click a card to pick it up, then click where it goes, or drag it. Double-click sends a card to Done, or to a free Cell. Once every column is in order, the board finishes itself.
  • Your wins, your win streak and your best time are kept.
